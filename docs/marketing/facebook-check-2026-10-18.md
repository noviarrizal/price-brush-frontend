## Summary for the CEO (read this first)

1. **Create the Facebook account: yes, now (Sun 18 – Tue 20/10), for groups only.** It costs Rp0 and about 30 minutes. Account age and time in a group are the only things that can't be sped up later. Admins can filter or auto-decline by both ([Meta, 2021](https://about.fb.com/news/2021/06/new-tools-to-help-group-admins-manage-their-communities/)). An account made today is about 5 weeks old by launch week.
2. **Groups: worth a small, capped try. They are still the densest free decorator channel I can find.** [fact] Decorators Forum UK's Page has **122,501 followers** (Facebook's public preview, 17/10). Snippets of Facebook's "related groups" lists show **Decorators Forum ~24–25K members**, **Spraying Makes Sense ~15K** and **Trade Decorator Forum 7,851**. One private group, **Painter & Decorator's Community UK**, describes itself as "for best or new products, quotes, pricing, **software**, work questions". But the evidence on outsiders is discouraging:
   - The Decorators Forum's main group is described as "No pricing posts though, they don't tend to go down too well!!"
   - Another group says "no advertising or selling in the group".
   - A member of a third complains about "irrelevant content (cuddly toys and shit for sale)".
   - Admins can filter join requests by **location** and request age ([Meta](https://www.facebook.com/help/617811410580927/)), so an Indonesian non-decorator will be refused by some trade-only groups.
3. **Plan:**
   - **Oct (≈ 50 min):** join 5 groups with honest answers and log their rules.
   - **1–21 Nov build weeks:** **0 required hours**, optionally ≤ 15 min/week on the phone.
   - **Thu 26/11:** **one** post in **one** group, only if its rules or an admin allow it. Put the free calculator first, ask decorators to correct the defaults, and leave the £49 offer out of the post.
   - **Total to 31/12:** ≈ 3 h.
4. **Meta ads: no, £0 in 2026 (same answer as before, for new reasons).**
   - [fact] Meta needs a **Facebook Page**: "Every advertiser will need to have a Facebook Page created before they can advertise" ([Meta](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/975570072950669)).
   - [fact] For link-click or landing-page-view optimisation, **"Advantage+ detailed targeting automatically applied with no option to opt out"** ([Meta](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/440167386536513)). A "painter and decorator" audience will not stay decorators.
   - What the money buys: Rp400k is about **£14.7 of media** after estimated VAT and fees, so **~10–29 clicks** and **~0.005–0.3 Founding sales** [estimate].
   - Worst of all, **a restriction on a new profile also blocks the groups**, which are the part that's actually worth something ([Meta](https://www.facebook.com/business/help/975570072950669)).
5. **What it changes:**
   - Launch week gains one row: **Thu 26/11 Facebook group post (conditional)**.
   - The expected 31/12 result moves from **0–2 Founding sales (centre 0–1)** to **0–3 (centre still 0–1)**, with a somewhat better chance of ≥ 1 payment [estimate].
   - No money moves. The ~Rp400k stays unspent.
6. **Strongest counter-argument:** each trade group I could see either sells promotion (sponsors, advertising), bans it, or dislikes pricing talk. An outsider's tool post may be ignored or mocked, and the hours and stress could go to SEO pages instead. **My answer:** the cap is ~3 h, and nothing is posted unless a group's rules or an admin say yes. If no group says yes by 21/11, the account becomes read-only research for `messaging.md` and nothing else is spent.

### A3. Page or personal profile?

- [fact] A Page can post in groups: "If you have Facebook access to a Page, you can act as the Page… If you can't join or participate in a group as a Page, it might be because the admins don't allow Pages in the group" ([help](https://www.facebook.com/help/215443548960829/)).
- **Recommendation: post as the personal profile, and create no Page in 2026.** [opinion] A "PriceBrush" Page posting in a trade group looks like an advert, and admins can bar Pages. A Page is only needed for ads, which I don't recommend (B). The profile bio carries the disclosure.

### A4. Can Arif post credibly? Is "I built a tool for decorators" accepted?

**Honest answer: in most of these groups it will read as a vendor post from an outsider. It may be accepted in one or two, only with permission and only framed as "correct my defaults".**

Evidence:
- **Trade communities are wary of outsiders' links:**
  - Screwfix Community: "If you register on the site and your first post is linking to a company… you will be treated with suspicion" (`channels-and-ads-2026-10-18.md` A2).
  - Reddit trade subs: "Replying to ai slop." (memo 004).
  - The groups above: "No pricing posts", "no advertising or selling", sponsor models, complaints about sales posts.
- **Trade press shows decorator founders get the warm angle.** PDN headlined "Painter & decorator launches PaintPilot" (05/10/2026). Paint Manager (launched 05/06/2026, "free for the first five quotes", £19.99–£39.99 a month) was pitched through its founder's "20 years" in construction ([Oswestry Life](https://www.oswestry.life/article/new-tool-launched-to-help-decorators-save-time-and-price-jobs-accurately/)). Arif can't claim that, and must never imply it (no invented credentials).
- **What works for an outsider [opinion, no direct evidence because groups are login-only]:** asking to be corrected rather than selling. "I'm not a decorator, so tell me where my defaults are wrong" invites the thing trade groups enjoy (correcting someone) and is true. It must also be a free tool, posted once, in a group that invites software talk.

Rules for Arif:
- Disclose in every comment that touches software: "I build PriceBrush, so I'm biased."
- Never pretend to be a decorator, and never give trade technique advice.
- Answer only maths (litres, m², tins) and software questions.

- 5–25% is the range from `channels-and-ads-2026-10-18.md` B3.

**Before launch, for the "tell me" list:** a visitor must use the calculator, open the quote template, download, then email "App". At the `funnel.md` targets (40% × 5% × 30% × an unknown email share), 10–29 clicks give **≈ 0 emails**.

**Bottom line:** every row loses money, even at the optimistic end. And 2–29 clicks can't tell us anything statistically.

### B4. Timing, payday and a stop rule (only if the founder overrides me)

- **Never before the app is live.** No money before 23/10 anyway. Pre-launch clicks can't buy (above).
- **If at all:** Wed 25/11 – Sun 29/11, from **October pay** (keep Rp100k aside from 23/10). Prepaid **GoPay/OVO top-up of exactly Rp100,000** in an IDR ad account (Meta lists them for Indonesia). The top-up is the hard cap, and nothing is left behind to forfeit.
- **Setup:** Traffic → landing page views; UK; ages 25–60; `?ref=meta`; no pixel (it would conflict with "Nothing you type leaves this browser").
- **Stop rule:** stop after Rp50,000 spent if the average CPC is over Rp25,000 (≈ £1.04) **or** there are 0 calculator uses from `?ref=meta`. Hard stop at Rp100,000. **No second top-up in 2026.** If the ad account or profile gets any restriction notice, stop and don't appeal through third-party "recovery" services.
- **Cost of the override:** creating a Page plus an ad account on the same new profile that we need for groups [opinion]. A profile restriction would close the groups too (B1). That's why I recommend no ads, not just small ads.

### B5. Meta vs Google Ads at the same budget

| | Meta | Google Search |
|---|---|---|
| Intent | None. Interrupts the feed | Searching now, but the buyer phrases barely exist in UK autocomplete (`channels-and-ads-2026-10-18.md` B3) |
| Targeting | Forced expansion on click goals (fact above) | Exact match. Google pauses "low search volume" keywords |
| CPC (third-party) | ~£0.50–£1.50 | ~£1.55–£3.65 |
| Clicks for Rp400k | 10–29 | ~6–14 (before VAT; not re-checked) |
| Extra setup | Page + ad account on a new profile; ad review | Google Ads account; Indonesian card **not verified** |
| Side risk | Can take the **groups** profile down with it | None to other channels |
| Verdict | **No** | **No in 2026.** CEO: decide a capped test in January 2027 only if Search Console shows buyer-query impressions |

### B6. Strongest argument for Meta ads, and what would change my mind

- **For:** decorators live on Facebook (122,501 Page followers). Meta clicks are cheaper than Google's, and ads are the only way to reach them without an admin's permission. £16 is cheap tuition.
- **My answer:** forced expansion means we don't know they'd be decorators. 10–29 clicks teach nothing. And it risks the profile that the free channel needs.
- **Would change my mind:**
  1. Ads Manager shows a UK "Painter and decorator" job-title audience, and a Reach/Awareness objective (where controls can stay strict) estimates ≥ 2,000 people reached for ≤ Rp100k.
  2. **And** the groups have already said no, so the profile has nothing to lose.

  Then run a Rp100k reach test in **January 2027**, after the 25/12 payday, outside the Founding launch week.
