## 1. Launch lead on 31 Oct: paint calculator (multi-room + labour/price) → "Turn this into a quote"; quote template 31 Oct; invoice 14 Nov

**Recommendation: Agree (memo 001, option B).** One condition: on 31 Oct, "Turn this into a quote" must open `/decorating-quote-template` already filled in, with one line per room and surface, and the line text must show the working (e.g. "Living room walls · 2 coats · 38 m² · 0.8 day — £160.00"). If that handover slips, launch both pages anyway, but put the hours into the handover before anything else.

**Why (checked independently)**
- [fact] Template SERPs are full: all of the "painting and decorating quote template uk" top 10 are small quoting SaaS pages, and one of them, Love Invoicing, already advertises "Prep, Coats, Materials" (`../seo/data/serp-ddg-uk-2026-10-16.md`, lines 65–75).
- [fact] The "paint calculator multiple rooms" results are US gallon calculators: ultimatefinancecalculator.com and homeimprovcalc.com (same file, lines 53–61).
- [fact] Paint is the biggest autocomplete cluster: 1,048 rows in `paint.csv`, against 410 in `quotes.csv` and 413 in `invoices.csv`.
- [fact] Pre-MVP there's no account to sign up for (paid MVP builds 1–21 Nov, PRD §9). So on 31 Oct the calculator's only conversion step *is* the quote template. That is the strongest reason to ship both together.

**Strongest argument against:** The calculator head term belongs to B&Q, Dulux and Wickes (`../seo/CHANGELOG.md` v0.2), and the trade long-tails have unknown, probably tiny volume. Most calculator users will be homeowners (memo 004: all 16 decorator-mentioning UK Reddit posts were by homeowners). We may rank for traffic that never buys. Sleepless Tradesman already has a multi-room paint tool with prep lines (`../strategy/competitors.md`).

**What would change my mind:** If by 15/12 `quote_prefilled ÷ calc_used` < 2% (memo 001's threshold) **and** `calc_room_added ÷ calc_used` < 5% [estimate, my threshold], move December hours to the quote template and the day-rate guide.

**Risks:** homeowners use the labour/price output to beat their decorator down on price [opinion]. To counter it, label the price "Your price at your day rate", which only makes sense to a trade user.

**What to change (proposed, not applied)**
- PRD §4 Flow A step 4: replace "Quote by voice in 60 seconds — try free" with **"Turn this into a quote"**. Before the MVP is live, the secondary line under it reads "Free, no sign-up. Saving and sending on WhatsApp comes later in November."

## 2. Headline + differentiator

**Recommendation: Keep the differentiator (decorator maths visible in the quote). Change the headline for launch.**

**Final (exact copy)**
- Keyword line above the H1: **Quote and invoice app for UK painters and decorators**
- **H1: Room sizes in. Priced quote out.**
- **Sub: Coats, litres, days and labour worked out from your day rate. Every number shown, every line yours to change. Made for UK decorators.**
- Primary CTA, 31 Oct until the MVP is live: **"Price a room free"** → `/paint-calculator`. Caption: "No sign-up. Works on any phone or laptop."
- Primary CTA after the MVP: **"Start a free quote"**. Caption: "Free for 5 quotes or invoices a month. No card needed."

**Alternatives**
1. **"Measure the rooms. Get the price right."** Sub as above. It leans on the underpricing pain and the trade's own verb ("price a job").
2. **"Say the rooms. Get a priced quote."** (memo 002). Use it only as the heading of the voice section once voice works, not as the H1.

**Why I disagree with "Say the rooms" as the H1**
- [fact] **It promises voice, which won't exist on 31 Oct.** The voice quote ships in the paid MVP, 1–21 Nov (PRD §9). Design's landing CTA is "Try it free — say a job" (`../design/screens.md` line 35). For three weeks, the page's first promise would be one visitors can't try. Our rule is "claims must be provable by the product".
- [fact] **"Say the …" is close to Fixxa's copy.** fixxa.ai (pulled 17/10): "Say the job in a voice note and turn it into a quote or invoice." Voice is also claimed by Jobber, VoxTrade, PainterPal and TradeQuoteAI (`../strategy/competitors.md`).
- [fact] **"Get a … quote" is homeowner language.** Bark: "Get free quotes". Sleepless's own homepage links homeowners with "Need a tradesman? Get a free quote →" (both pulled 17/10). The calculator will draw homeowners (memo 004), and "Get a priced quote" reads to them like a quote-request service.

**Competitor check for my headline (17/10)**

| Phrase | Found? |

| "Room sizes in" / "Priced quote out" | Not found by WebSearch (US-biased; **Google UK not verified**: CAPTCHA, per `../seo/CHANGELOG.md`) |

| "before you leave site" | Sleepless ("ready before you leave site") and VoxTrade. Avoid |
| "Quote. Win. Get Paid." / "in minutes" | Paint Manager (paintmanager.co.uk meta, new UK decorator tool, £19.99–£39.99). Avoid |
| "priced lines" | QuoteFlow (alternativeto.net listing). Ours is "priced quote", different enough [opinion] |

**UK decorator language behind it:** [fact] Autocomplete has "how to price a painting job", "how to price up a painting job uk" and "painter decorator price per room" (`../seo/data/*.csv`). In memo 004's r/Plastering thread: "price is a day rate plus materials". On UK Business Forums (thread 19/03–06/10/2026), trades talk about "labour, materials, markup" and "description of works", and the phrases "price up" and "day rate" didn't appear there. "Rooms", "day rate", "coats" and "price" are our words. "Litres" goes in the sub, not the H1.

**Strongest argument against my headline:** "X in. Y out." is a slightly engineered slogan form, and it drops voice, the most demo-able thing we have. Leading with the maths makes us look like "a calculator", not "an app". Memo 002's line is warmer and more human.

**What would change my mind:** (a) a 5-second test with 5+ decorators on The Decorators Forum: show both heroes and ask "what does this do, and who is it for?" (free, async); (b) once both are live, GSC CTR on the home page title/description. If voice tests well with decorators in late November, swap to "Say the rooms…" then. By that point it is provable.

**Risks:** "Priced quote out" could still read as instant or automatic [opinion]. The sub's "every line yours to change" handles that.

**What to change (proposed)**

## 6. Show prices "incl. VAT"

**Recommendation: Agree.** Show **"£9 a month, incl. VAT"**. At checkout and in the paywall terms: "You'll pay £9 today (incl. VAT), then £9 every month."

**Why (checked):**
- [fact] Polar's default "Location-based" tax setting gives UK customers **inclusive** pricing: "The price shown to the customer already includes tax." ([polar.sh docs](https://polar.sh/docs/features/tax-inclusive-pricing)). So if we list £9, the UK buyer pays £9, and writing "+ VAT" would be wrong.
- [fact] The main persona (Dave) is not VAT registered (PRD §2), so VAT is a real cost to him. "incl. VAT" is the true price, while Powered Now shows "£28 … ex VAT" (`../strategy/competitors.md`). [estimate] For a non-registered decorator, that's £33.60 against our £9.

**Strongest argument against:** **Margin.** [estimate] £9 incl. VAT is £7.50 net. After Polar's new-organisation fee of 5% + $0.50 ([polar.sh blog, 20/05/2026](https://polar.sh/blog/introducing-polar-plans); exact current fee **not verified**, docs conflict), that's about **£6.70 per month**, and about £38 from a £49 Founding Member sale. Also, B2B software is usually shown ex VAT, and VAT-registered users (Sophie) think ex VAT.

**What would change my mind:** If the founder needs £9 *net*, set Polar to "Exclusive" and show **"£9 + VAT (£10.80)"**. That is honest, but it gives up the cleaner "£9" against Queezy's £8.99.

**Not verified:** whether Polar is registered for UK VAT itself (the docs mention EU OSS only), and whether VAT-registered UK buyers can enter a VAT number to get a VAT invoice. Check in the Polar dashboard before opening checkout.

**What to change:** PRD §9 open question → closed: "incl. VAT". Set Polar's tax behaviour **explicitly to Inclusive**, not left on Location-based, so a later default change can't break the copy. `../seo/keyword-map.md` `/pricing` meta: proposed "Free for 5 quotes or invoices a month. Pro £9 a month incl. VAT, or £79 a year. Founding Member £49 once. No per-user fees." (126 chars; the SEO agent decides). Design paywall terms line as above.

## 7. Cancel = 1 tap + 1 confirmation

**Recommendation: Partly disagree.** We advertise **"Cancel in one tap"** in the PRD positioning, landing sub, promise strip, `/pricing` meta and paywall. Tap + confirm is two taps, and our brand is "honest about money" (`../design/design-system.md`). Do this instead:

- **Preferred:** Settings → Plan → **"Cancel plan"** (one tap) cancels at period end straight away. Toast: **"Cancelled. You keep Pro until 15/11/2026. [Undo]"**, and Settings shows **"Resume Pro"** until that date. A mistaken tap costs nothing: Pro keeps running to the end of the period, and Undo or Resume reverses it. That means the confirmation doesn't protect the user from anything. No retention offer and no survey wall; an optional one-tap "Why?" can appear *after* cancelling.
- **If you keep the confirmation:** change every "Cancel in one tap" to **"Cancel in two taps. No phone call."**

**Why:** [fact] Powered Now's 1-star reviews: "charges continued after cancelling", "surprise auto-renewal" (`../strategy/competitors.md`); Tradify reviews mention sales calls. Easy cancelling is a real proof point. [fact] The UK DMCC subscription rules (easy exit) are not in force yet. Most sources say spring 2027, and one says January 2027 ([Winston Taylor](https://www.winstontaylor.com/insights/dmcca-subscription-contracts-regime-faces-further-delay), [Acquired](https://www.acquired.com/blog/dmcca-subscription-legislation-january-2027/); exact date **not verified**). Being simple now costs nothing.

**Strongest argument against:** a confirmation is the norm and users expect it. Undo needs Polar to support un-cancelling a subscription set to cancel at period end (**not verified**; dev to check the Polar API). One extra tap is also a trivial "lie" that few would notice.
**What would change my mind:** If Polar can't un-cancel, keep the confirmation and use the "two taps" copy. Don't keep the claim and the confirmation together.

**What to change:** Design A12 Settings (`../design/screens.md` line 139): replace the confirm dialog with the toast + Resume, or change the copy. PRD F12 acceptance criteria: "Cancel = one tap; Undo available until period end."

## 8. Client page Accept button: PriceBrush yellow for MVP, own brand colour as Pro later

**Recommendation: Agree for MVP.** For Pro, bundle "your colours" into the existing Pro item "your own logo" (PRD §7). Don't market brand colour as a separate reason to pay.

**Why:** [fact] Design puts the decorator first on the client page (header with their name, then a summary card; pattern from YourTradebase, Stripe and Jobber, `../design/research.md`). So the homeowner sees Dave's business first, and the yellow button is just "the action". [opinion] The same yellow "Accept quote" across every decorator's quote makes the button familiar, and it works alongside the Free watermark as a quiet brand touch. [fact] Yellow on ink is 10.22:1 contrast (`../design/design-system.md`); a decorator's own colour might fail AA.

**Strongest argument against:** decorators who care about looking professional (persona Sophie, PRD §2: what makes her pay is looking professional) may dislike a third-party colour on *their* quote page. Colour customisation is cheap to build, and giving it to Free users could raise activation.
**What would change my mind:** If 3+ users ask for it unprompted, or if Pro conversion shows "logo/branding" is the reason people pay.

**Risks:** with a custom colour, a low-contrast choice could make Accept unreadable. Auto-check contrast and fall back to yellow.

**What to change:** PRD §7 Pro: "Your logo and colours on quotes and invoices" (post-MVP for colours). Design: note the contrast fallback rule.

## 9. WhatsApp: official logo per brand guidelines

**Recommendation: Agree: use the official WhatsApp glyph**, downloaded from Meta's WhatsApp brand resources (accept their terms when downloading; that's a download, not an upload). Rules from [Meta's WhatsApp brand page](https://www.meta.com/brand/resources/whatsapp/whatsapp-brand/) (opened 17/10/2026, quoted):

| Rule (quoted) | What it means for us |
|---|---|
| "Use the WhatsApp name and logos found on our WhatsApp Brand Guidelines website only" | No icon packs, no redrawn glyph |
| "You shouldn't modify any colors in our logos" / "may not modify … in any way" | Use the official colourway as supplied. Never tint it coach green, yellow or ink. If the official pack has a white or black variant, use that on the yellow button; otherwise put the green glyph on a white chip (**not verified** which variants exist; the page doesn't list them) |
| Logos may be scaled "as long as they are not the most distinctive or prominent feature" | Glyph at text size (20–24 px) next to the label; never a giant hero glyph |
| Display the word "WhatsApp" in "the same font size and style as the content surrounding it"; don't replace the word with the logo | Label is **"Send on WhatsApp"** in our button font. Never the glyph alone |
| "Avoid using the WhatsApp name or logos … as a verb" | Never "WhatsApp it", "WhatsApp Dave", "WhatsApp your client". Client page button: **"Message Dave on WhatsApp"** |
| "DON'T … imply partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement" | No "WhatsApp-powered" or "official partner". Fixxa *is* a "Meta Business Verified · Official WhatsApp Business Partner" (fixxa.ai); we aren't, so never echo it |
| Don't combine with other logos; not in product names or domains | No "PriceBrush for WhatsApp" names; no `whatsapp` in URLs |

**Strongest argument against:** design's current choice (Lucide `message-circle` plus the word "WhatsApp") has zero trademark risk. Meta "may revoke permission … at any time", and we'd need to follow a brand pack we haven't seen (clear space and minimum size aren't on the page, **not verified**).
**What would change my mind:** If the downloaded pack's terms forbid use in a share button (not stated on the page), keep the generic bubble and the word.

**Note:** Meta's page has no rules specific to share buttons (**not verified** beyond the general rules). WhatsApp's own "Chat on WhatsApp" button assets are for businesses' click-to-chat, not for a share action like ours.

**What to change:** `../design/design-system.md` §icons: replace the "open question" with these rules. `screens.md` client page "[WhatsApp]" → "Message Dave on WhatsApp". Store the downloaded asset in the repo with a note of the source and date.
