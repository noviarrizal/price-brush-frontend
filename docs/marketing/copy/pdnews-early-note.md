```
Hi Lucy,

A short news item for PDN readers, in case it's of use. I can email a screenshot if you'd like one.

Headline: PriceBrush launches free paint and labour calculator for decorators

PriceBrush has launched a free online calculator that helps painters and decorators work out the paint, time and price for a job from room sizes.

Decorators enter the size of each room and the number of coats. The calculator works out the wall area, the litres and tins needed, the days on the job and a price at the decorator's own day rate, and it shows every step of the sum.

[T16] A ceiling and a mist coat on new plaster can each be added as their own line.

The starting figures are 12 m² per litre, 40 m² a day, £6 a litre for paint and a £200 day rate. Every one of them can be changed.

A "Turn this into a quote" button moves the rooms into a free quote template, line by line, ready to edit and download as a PDF. Free PDFs carry a small "Made with PriceBrush" mark.

Both tools work in the browser on a phone or laptop, with no sign-up, and nothing typed into them leaves the browser.

PriceBrush is being built by Arif Noviarrizal, an independent software developer based in Indonesia.

Arif said: "I'm not a decorator, so I've made the calculator show every number rather than hide it. The defaults are my starting guesses, and I'd like decorators to tell me where they're wrong for the way they work."

The calculator is free to use at pricebrush.co.uk/paint-calculator.

Arif Noviarrizal
PriceBrush
pricebrush.app@gmail.com
```
