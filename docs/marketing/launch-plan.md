## Rules that hold every week

- In any community: say "I'm building PriceBrush", answer the actual question, and link only when the link is the answer. No surveys, no "what's your biggest headache?" (memo 004).
- Reddit is for homeowner paint and mist-coat answers only (r/DIYUK, following its rules), never for decorator recruitment.
- No numbers we can't prove (see `messaging.md`). No fake reviews. Ask Founding Members for a review with no incentive tied to the rating.

## If it's not working (decide on 15/12)

| Signal | Change |
|---|---|
| < 2% "Turn this into a quote" | Move December's hours to the quote template and day-rate guide (memo 001) |
| Listings bring visits but no sign-ups | Rewrite the hero (try alternative 1 in `messaging.md`) |
| Sign-ups but 0 payments by 20/12 | Email the activated users asking what stops them. Don't cut the price below £9 (Queezy £8.99 is the floor) |
