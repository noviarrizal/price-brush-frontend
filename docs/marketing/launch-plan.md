## Fact that changes the forum plan (checked 17/10/2026)

- **"The Decorators Forum" is now a Facebook group, not a message board.** `decoratorsforum.com` and `decoratorsforum.co.uk` both redirect to `facebook.com/groups/decoratorsforum` (curl, 17/10/2026). The group sits behind the Facebook login, so **its rules are not verified**.
- `thedecoratorsforum.com` (the address in `../seo/link-building.md`) is a WordPress blog with reviews and news. It has no place to post threads. Its terms cover "blog content, product reviews, comment sections" and say "Our trademarks and trade dress may not be used in connection with any product or service without the prior written consent of The Decorators Forum UK" ([terms](https://www.thedecoratorsforum.com/terms/)). Its contact page offers paid exposure ("Our website and social media platforms receive a huge amount of traffic") ([contact](https://www.thedecoratorsforum.com/contact/)). That is paid, so we don't use it.
- The about page describes "a community where real industry voices are heard… across several platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and YouTube" ([about](https://www.thedecoratorsforum.com/about/)).

**So:** the post goes in the Facebook group, but only after the founder has read the group's rules while logged in. He must also have admin permission if the rules require it or are unclear. **No permission means no post**, and there is no workaround (no "search PriceBrush", no link dropped in the comments, no blog-comment links).

**When it's sold out:** "All 50 Founding places have gone. Thank you." **After 31/01/2027:** remove it everywhere.

**Rules:** {n} always comes from real Polar orders, updated by hand. Never round it, start it below 50 or invent it. **Don't extend the deadline.** "Ends 31/01/2027" has to be true, or it is fake urgency. "Lifetime" is always written as "for as long as PriceBrush runs", because the PRD has a stop-or-pivot review on 30/04/2027.

**Strongest argument against the offer:** "lifetime" plus a possible stop on 30/04/2027 could feel like a bait to the first 50 buyers. It also caps revenue from the keenest users at about £37 net each (CEO memo #6). **Mitigation:** the 60-day notice and CSV export line (F13 export exists in the PRD). **Founder decision (17/10/2026): no refund guarantee.** The "£49 back if PriceBrush closes before 31/01/2028" line is NOT used: net receipt is ~£37 per sale and Polar keeps its fee on refunds, so each full refund would cost the founder ~£12 out of pocket (worst case 50 × £49 = £2,450) while he has no funds. The promise stays: 60 days' notice + CSV export.
**What would change my mind:** 0 Founding sales after launch week and the December email, with ≥ 4 activated users. That points to the offer or the audience, not the price. Ask the activated users what stops them before changing anything.
