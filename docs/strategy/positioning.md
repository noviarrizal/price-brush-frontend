## Who we are for

Solo or two-person **UK painters and decorators** who price by the room and the day. They quote from a cheap Android on site, or finish quotes on a laptop at home (PRD §2, §6.1). They are not homeowners and not trade teams of 5+.

## The one problem we solve better than anyone

**Turning room sizes into a price you can trust, then into a quote and an invoice, without doing the maths at night.** The litres, coats (including mist coat on new plaster), days and labour are worked out from *your* day rate, and every number is shown and can be changed. Nobody we read does this correctly *and* inside the quote (`competitors.md`, "What this table says").

## Messaging hierarchy

- **Headline:** "Say the rooms. Get a priced quote."
- **Sub:** "Paint, coats, days and labour worked out from your day rate, every number shown and editable. Made for UK decorators."
- **Supporting claims:**
  1. **Maths you can check.** "See every litre, coat and day before you send it." (Proof: the free calculator.)
  2. **Quote to invoice, same job.** "Send on WhatsApp. When it's accepted, it's an invoice." (Avoid "one tap / one click"; see below.)
  3. **No per-user fees.** "£9 a month, incl. VAT. No per-user fees. No sales calls. Cancel in one tap." (Counters Tradify per-user pricing and sales calls, and Powered Now's billing complaints.)

**Alternative headlines** (use if the founder prefers not to lead with voice):

- "Room sizes in. Priced quote out."
- "Price every room right: paint, days and labour, worked out."
- "The quote app that does the decorating maths."

## Words to avoid

- "AI-powered", "revolutionary", "all-in-one", "platform", "solution", "estimate entity": generic, or US/SaaS words (PRD §6.6).
- "Estimate" in headlines (a US word; body copy only, `../seo/keyword-research.md` §2.3).
- "Free forever" (the Free plan has limits; say "Free for 5 documents a month").
- Any number we can't prove yet: hours saved, quote acceptance rates, user counts.
- "MTD-ready" or "HMRC-recognised": not true before 2027.
