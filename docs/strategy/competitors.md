**How to read this**

- **Threat** means threat to the 31 Dec 2026 goal: does this product compete for the same UK decorator searches *and* the same solo buyer?
- **Ratings** are from the UK App Store (`itunes.apple.com/lookup?...&country=gb`).
- **Prices:** some sites showed prices in SGD or USD to our fetcher, which may have been geolocated outside the UK. Those are marked "GBP unknown".
- "Unknown" means we didn't find it. It does not mean "none".

## What this table says

1. "Fast", "voice", "AI", "one tap to invoice", "no sign-up" and "5 free a month" are all taken (memo 002).
2. The two **High** threats both use **free decorator content and tools**, the same channel we use. Our chance against them is accuracy you can see and the depth of the calculator → quote path (memos 001 and 002).
3. Almost all the decorator-only apps are **iPhone only or not yet launched** (Queezy, PainterPal, Kvota). Working on Android and in a desktop browser is a real, if small, advantage.
4. Nobody publishes revenue. TrustMRR wasn't checked, because we don't know any competitor's slug. Traction for all of them is unknown beyond the ratings counts above.
