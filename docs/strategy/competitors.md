# Competitors (v0.3)

Date: 17/10/2026 · Author: strategist · v0.1 rows were checked on 16/10/2026. The v0.2 checks (prices and traction) ran on 16–17/10/2026; the "Checked" column shows the date per row.

**How to read this**

- **Threat** means threat to the 31 Dec 2026 goal: does this product compete for the same UK decorator searches *and* the same solo buyer?
- **Ratings** are from the UK App Store (`itunes.apple.com/lookup?...&country=gb`, 17/10/2026).
- **Price confidence:**
  - **verified**: seen on a first-party page in GBP (the vendor's own site or structured data, or the vendor's in-app purchases on the GB App Store).
  - **secondary**: a dated third-party page quoting £.
  - **unverified**: no dated £ source.
- **App Store IAP prices** are what Apple charges UK users inside the app. They are often higher than the vendor's web price, and VAT is included. The web price is the fairer comparison for a browser product like ours.
- **Revenue:** no competitor publishes revenue. All 13 TrustMRR slugs we tried returned 404; a control slug (`shoptok`) returned 200, so the URL format is right and these products simply aren't listed. Companies House "total exemption" and micro-entity accounts don't include turnover. The column below gives the best traction proxy instead and never an estimate.
- "Unknown" means we didn't find it. It does not mean "none".
- Full method, dead ends and sources: `decisions/004-verification-results.md`.

## What this table says

1. "Fast", "voice", "AI", "one tap to invoice", "no sign-up" and "5 free a month" are all taken (memo 002). Unchanged.
2. The two **High** threats both use **free decorator content and tools**, the same channel we use. v0.2 confirms both are weak as products: Sleepless has 3 GB ratings, its newest review is 1-star and it hasn't shipped since July; TradeQuoteAI hard-codes "15" users. They are content rivals, not product rivals. Our chance against them is still accuracy you can see and the depth of the calculator → quote path (memos 001 and 002).
3. **On price, £9 flat is at the bottom of the ladder** with Queezy (iPhone only, quote-capped) and TradeQuoteAI (USD only). Every incumbent charges at least £25.99 for one user. So "no per-user fees" is now verified against Tradify, Powered Now and Jobber.
4. **Fixxa is the only competitor with a sign of outside money** (four share allotments in 2026). Its review activity stopped after launch week. Watch it in January, when MTD marketing starts.
5. Nobody publishes revenue, and none of these products is on TrustMRR. The best traction proxies are rating counts and review velocity. The incumbents get few new written UK reviews: Tradify 4 in 90 days, Powered Now none in 11 months. Every new entrant has 0–8 ratings.
