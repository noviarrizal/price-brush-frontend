Questions decorators ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The paint calculator and the quote template are free, with no sign-up and no card.

Do you keep what I type?

No. Both tools work in your browser. Nothing you type leaves it.

Do I need to be VAT registered?

No. Leave VAT off. If you are registered, turn VAT on in the quote template and we add 20%.

Can I make an invoice?

Not yet. The free invoice template is coming soon. The app, where an accepted quote becomes an invoice, comes in late November.
