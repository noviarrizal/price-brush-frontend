How we work it out

  1. Walls: 2 × (length + width) × height.
  2. Take off 1.8 m² per door and 1.5 m² per window.
  3. Add the ceiling (length × width) if ticked.
  4. Multiply by coats, divide by coverage (12 m² per litre is typical for emulsion — check your tin).
  5. Add 10% spare and round up to real tin sizes (10 L, 5 L, 2.5 L).

No tape measure? A 4 × 4 m room usually needs about 5 litres for two coats on the walls.
