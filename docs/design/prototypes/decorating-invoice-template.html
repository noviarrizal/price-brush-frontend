What should be on a UK invoice?

  • The word "Invoice" and a unique number
  • Your business name, address and contact details
  • Your client's name and address
  • What you did, the date the work finished, and the invoice date
  • Each amount and the total due; VAT and your VAT number if registered

Source: GOV.UK, "Invoicing and taking payment from customers". Not sure about VAT? Do decorators charge VAT?
