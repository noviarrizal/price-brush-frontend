## Seasonality (what we know vs. assume)

- **Known dates:** Self Assessment online deadline 31 Jan; new tax year 6 Apr; MTD for Income Tax over £30,000 from 6 Apr 2027 (over £50,000 since 6 Apr 2026; over £20,000 from Apr 2028 — GOV.UK). Publish tax content **4–8 weeks before** each date so it's indexed in time.
- **Assumptions (check in Google Trends, free, 5 years, UK):** paint-quantity searches likely peak around spring and the Christmas/New Year period, exterior paint in summer; trade software interest likely peaks in January (new-year admin). Founder: check "paint calculator", "how much paint do i need", "invoice template" in Google Trends UK and note the peaks here.

## Content rules (all pages)

- UK English, £, VAT, HMRC; "quote" not "estimate".
- Every number has a source or is labelled as our calculation/assumption.
- "Last reviewed" date on tax/legal pages; review every April.
- Real author on guides (the founder, with a true bio). No fake experts, no fake reviews.
- One primary keyword per URL (`keyword-map.md`); check before writing anything new.
